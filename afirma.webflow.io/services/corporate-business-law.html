FAQ

Common Questions

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Please chat to our friendly team or write us an email at info@afirma.com

  • What areas of law does your firm specialize in?

    Our firm specializes in a wide range of legal areas, including corporate law, family law, real estate, criminal defense, personal injury, and intellectual property. We provide tailored legal solutions to meet the unique needs of individuals and businesses.

  • How do I schedule a consultation with your firm?

    You can schedule a consultation by filling out the contact form on our website, calling our office directly, or sending us an email. Once we receive your inquiry, a member of our team will get in touch with you to confirm the date and time.

  • What should I bring to my first legal consultation?

    Please bring all relevant documents related to your case, such as contracts, correspondence, court notices, or any paperwork you believe may help us understand your situation better. A written summary of your case or questions is also helpful.

  • How does your billing process work?

    Our billing process depends on the nature of your case. We offer hourly rates, flat fees for specific services, and contingency fee arrangements in certain cases like personal injury. During the initial consultation, we will clearly explain our fees and payment structure.

  • Do I really need a lawyer for my legal issue?

    While not all legal issues require an attorney, having legal representation ensures your rights are protected and your case is handled properly. A lawyer can help you avoid costly mistakes, provide expert advice, and represent you effectively in negotiations or court proceedings.
